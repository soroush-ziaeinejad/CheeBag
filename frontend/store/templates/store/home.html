Your home, in the bag.

CheeBag is a demo home and kitchen store built around CHEE, an AI assistant powered by Claude. CHEE works with the live store: it finds products, tracks your orders and can cancel one, but only after you click Confirm. The products and reviews are real (from Amazon); the customers and orders are simulated.
